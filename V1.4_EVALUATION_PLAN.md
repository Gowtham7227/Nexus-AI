## 2. EVALUATION DIMENSIONS & BENCHMARK METRICS

### A. Retrieval Performance
- **Hit@K (K=3, 5, 10)**: Proportion of queries where all necessary bridge chunks are present in top-$K$.
- **Recall@K**: Percentage of gold evidence chunks captured across multi-document corpus.
- **Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)**: Evaluates ranking position of primary target facts.
- **Source & Page Accuracy**: Precision of attributing facts to the exact document and page number.

### B. Answer Quality & Grounding
- **Grounded Claim Ratio ($GCR$)**: $GCR = \frac{\text{Supported Claims}}{\text{Total Claims Generated}}$ (Target $\ge 95\%$).
- **Citation Precision & Recall**: Fraction of numerical citations `[1]` that directly substantiate the preceding sentence.
- **Unsupported Claim Ratio**: Fraction of generated sentences lacking textual grounding in the retrieved context.

### C. Multi-Document Reasoning & Conflict Detection
- **Multi-Hop Step Accuracy**: Accuracy of intermediate reasoning steps during iterative decomposition.
- **Contradiction Detection F1-Score**: Harmonic mean of precision and recall in flagging opposing claims between Document A and Document B.
- **Temporal Attribution Score**: Ability to distinguish older superseded versions from active policy/document versions.

### D. System Latency & Resource Efficiency
- **Time-to-First-Token (TTFT)**: SSE latency benchmark under single-hop vs multi-hop modes.
- **End-to-End Latency**: Total request completion time (Budget: Single-hop $<1.2\text{s}$, Multi-hop $<3.5\text{s}$).
- **Token Overhead**: Extra input/output tokens incurred by iterative sub-query formulation.

## 5. ACADEMIC & TECHNICAL POSITIONING

### Suggested Title:
*"NexusAI: A Self-Verifying Multi-Document RAG Architecture with Iterative Query Decomposition and Cross-Source Contradiction Resolution"*

### Problem Statement:
Standard single-pass Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models suffer from severe degradation when queries require bridging intermediate facts across multiple documents or when ingested documents contain conflicting claims. Existing systems either average contradictory evidence into hallucinated compromise answers or fail to retrieve the complete chain of necessary facts.

### Concrete System Contributions:
1. **Bounded Iterative Query Decomposition**: A deterministic, sub-millisecond sufficiency controller that formulates targeted sub-queries without unbounded recursive LLM execution.
2. **Dual-Citation Contradiction Attribution**: An evidence verification engine that identifies opposing factual claims across ingested sources and presents explicit contrastive grounding to the generative model.
3. **Air-Gapped Local/Cloud Hybrid Pipeline**: Enterprise-grade multi-tenant RAG architecture with sub-millisecond parameter optimization and complete local offline execution capabilities.

## 7. RISK MITIGATION STRATEGY

- **Latency Inflation Risk**: Multi-hop retrieval involves multiple search iterations.
  - *Mitigation*: Hard cap iterations at 3 hops; execute parallel searches for independent sub-clauses; skip multi-hop for single-document simple factual queries via `rag_optimizer.py`.
- **False Conflict Risk**: Overly aggressive contradiction detection could flag complementary statements as conflicts.
  - *Mitigation*: Enforce high Cross-Encoder NLI threshold ($\ge 0.85$) before emitting contradiction tags.
- **Zero-Hit Fallback**: Sub-queries that yield zero hits must not break the global pipeline.
  - *Mitigation*: Graceful degradation returning partial accumulated evidence.
