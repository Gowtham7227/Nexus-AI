## 5. THREE CORE RESEARCH CONTRIBUTION THEMES

### Theme 1: Self-Verifying Multi-Hop Iterative RAG
- **Core Concept**: When a complex query cannot be answered by a single retrieval pass, an iterative decomposition controller formulates sub-queries, evaluates evidence sufficiency at each step, and halts once sufficient information is gathered.
- **Novelty Boundary**: While multi-hop RAG exists in academic literature (e.g., Self-RAG, DSPy, ReAct), NexusAI integrates a deterministic, sub-millisecond sufficiency evaluator that avoids expensive infinite LLM loops while preserving strict tenant isolation and XML grounding.

### Theme 2: Cross-Document Contradiction Detection & Discrepancy Attribution
- **Core Concept**: When multiple ingested documents contain opposing factual statements (e.g., differing financial forecasts, policy versions, or dates), the system explicitly flags the contradiction, attributes each claim to its exact source and page, and avoids generating a misleading blend.
- **Novelty Boundary**: Most RAG systems average or pick the highest-ranked chunk. NexusAI provides dual-citation contrastive containment (`<contradiction claim_a="..." source_a="..." claim_b="..." source_b="..." />`).

### Theme 3: Privacy-Bounded Multi-Tier Model Routing
- **Core Concept**: Real-time evaluation of document sensitivity and query privacy risk governing an automatic local-first vs cloud dispatch decision, ensuring zero PII or restricted corporate data reaches external APIs while maintaining high response quality for non-sensitive tasks.
- **Novelty Boundary**: Direct integration between regex/heuristic privacy scanning, document tenant access metadata, and model execution pipelines.

## 6. RECOMMENDATION FOR v1.4

To maintain maximum engineering focus, architectural cleanliness, and verifiable experimental value, we recommend selecting **Theme 1 (Multi-Hop Iterative Retrieval & Self-Verification)** and **Theme 2 (Evidence Contradiction Detection)** as the core research contributions, accompanied by **Table-Aware Structured Chunking** as a supporting product feature.
